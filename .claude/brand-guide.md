# Zebri Brand Guide

## Overview

**Zebri** is a minimal CRM built specifically for Wedding MCs — the professionals who run wedding ceremonies and receptions. It replaces spreadsheets, sticky notes, and scattered emails with a calm, focused control panel for managing every couple from first enquiry to post-wedding follow-up.

**Brand personality:** Minimal. Fast. Calm. Modern. Never enterprise.

**Who it's for:** Independent Wedding MCs who want to feel organised and professional without drowning in software.

**Workflow it supports:** Enquiry → Quote → Booking → Wedding Day → Follow-up

---

## Logo & Mark

### Full Logo
- File: `zebri-logo.svg`
- Dimensions: 500 × 180px (landscape)
- Usage: Website headers, email signatures, printed materials, anywhere there's horizontal space

### Icon Mark
- File: `zebri-icon.svg`
- Dimensions: 320 × 320px (square)
- Usage: App icon, social media profile images, favicons, small contexts where the full wordmark won't read

### Logo Rules
- Always use the official SVG files — never recreate or retype the wordmark
- The logo is pure **black (#111111) on white** — do not recolor it
- On dark backgrounds: use a white version with sufficient contrast
- Minimum clear space: equal to the height of the "Z" mark on all sides
- Do not stretch, rotate, add drop shadows, or apply effects to the logo
- Do not place the logo on busy photographic backgrounds without a clean container

| Status | Color |

- **Never use more than 2 brand colors in a single composition**
- Black and white are the dominant palette — mint is an accent only
- Use mint green for small highlights, not large fills or backgrounds

- No color overlays on photography

## Typography

### Primary Typeface
**Inter** — available free on [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter)

Fallback stack: `system-ui, sans-serif`

### Type Hierarchy

| Role | Weight | Size guidance |
|------|--------|---------------|
| Display / Hero headlines | Semibold (600) | Large, confident — 40px+ |
| Section headings | Semibold (600) | 24–32px |
| Subheadings / labels | Medium (500) | 14–18px |
| Body copy | Regular (400) | 14–16px |
| Captions / metadata | Regular (400) | 12–13px |

### Typography Rules
- Default to Inter Regular for all body copy
- Headlines should feel large and clean — let size do the work, not weight alone
- All-caps sparingly, only for short labels or tags
- Line height: comfortable and generous — 1.5× for body, 1.2× for headings
- Letter spacing: default (no tight tracking on body text)
- Avoid mixing more than 2 weights in a single design

## Voice & Tone

### Personality
Calm. Direct. Competent. Like a seasoned professional who's done this a hundred times.

### Writing Principles
- **Short sentences.** No filler.
- **Functional language** — talk about what the MC actually does (books couples, confirms events, sends quotes)
- **Never generic CRM-speak** — avoid words like "leads", "pipeline", "opportunities", "synergies"
- **Wedding-specific vocabulary** — use: enquiry, couple, booking, wedding day, follow-up, ceremony, reception
- **Reassuring, not salesy** — Zebri is a calm tool, not a hype product

### Example Tone

| Avoid | Prefer |
|-------|--------|
| "Manage your leads in the pipeline" | "See every couple at a glance" |
| "Optimize your workflow" | "Less time on admin. More time at the mic." |
| "Powerful CRM features" | "Everything a Wedding MC actually needs" |
| "Unlock your potential" | "Stay organised from first enquiry to final bow" |

### Writing for Social Media
- Lead with a specific, relatable moment (packing up after a wedding, replying to an enquiry at 11pm)
- Short posts perform better — one strong idea per post
- Questions and prompts work well for engagement
- Hashtags: keep minimal, relevant to wedding industry professionals

## Design Principles

### For All Creative Work

1. **Whitespace is intentional** — generous padding signals calm and quality
2. **Black, white, and mint** — the three-color palette is the brand's visual fingerprint
3. **Clean geometry** — prefer straight lines, sharp corners, minimal ornamentation
4. **No gradients** — flat color only
5. **No decorative drop shadows** — a single subtle shadow for depth on UI elements only
6. **Minimal iconography** — line icons with a 1.5px stroke weight (Lucide icon style)
7. **Typography-led** — let the type carry the design; resist the urge to add visual noise

### Layouts
- Content-first: the layout should serve the information, not the other way around
- Align to a grid; avoid random positioning
- Group related elements; leave space between groups
- On mobile: simplify further — one column, larger touch targets

## Photography & Imagery

### Style
- Clean, editorial wedding photography
- Light and airy — bright backgrounds, natural light preferred
- Candid moments over posed shots
- Focus on the MC experience: behind the scenes, at the mic, with couples

### What Works
- Black and white photography pairs perfectly with the brand palette
- Close crops with strong composition
- Authentic, real-wedding imagery over stock photography

### What to Avoid
- Busy, cluttered backgrounds
- Oversaturated or heavily filtered images
- Generic stock photography of office workers or laptops
- Dark, moody photography (clashes with the calm brand tone)

### Overlaying Text on Images
- Only overlay text on clean, low-detail areas of an image
- Use a white or semi-transparent container behind text for legibility
- Never use the brand's mint or black as a full-image overlay

## Social Media Guidelines

### Profile Images
- Use the **Zebri icon mark** (`zebri-icon.svg`) — black on white, clean square format

### Post Graphics
- Stick to the brand palette: black, white, mint green, and neutrals
- Use Inter font for any typographic treatments
- Keep text minimal — one clear message per graphic
- Generous whitespace in every composition
- Avoid template-heavy designs; keep it clean and editorial

### Content Pillars
1. **Product** — what Zebri does, new features, UI moments
2. **Workflow** — tips and insights for Wedding MCs on staying organised
3. **Community** — wedding industry moments, relatable MC experiences
4. **Social proof** — testimonials, before/after workflow stories

### Hashtag Strategy
Keep hashtags relevant and minimal. Examples:
- `#weddingmc` `#weddingceremonies` `#weddingprofessional`
- `#weddingbusiness` `#weddingplanning` `#mclife`

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Use lots of whitespace — it signals quality
- Let black and white dominate; use mint as a subtle accent
- Write in plain, direct language specific to Wedding MCs
- Use the official SVG logo files
- Keep designs simple and uncluttered

### Don't
- Don't use more than 2 brand colors in one design
- Don't add gradients or decorative effects to the logo
- Don't use generic CRM or SaaS language
- Don't crowd the layout — if it feels busy, remove something
- Don't retype or recreate the Zebri wordmark by hand
- Don't use mint green as a dominant background color
